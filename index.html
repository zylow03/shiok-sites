Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before claiming a slot.

Is it really free?

Yes. During our launch promo, the design and build of your website cost nothing. Slots are limited, and the promo ends once they're filled.

Is there anything I'll need to pay for?

You may need your own domain name (like yourbusiness.com) and a hosting or website-builder plan, paid directly to those providers. We'll guide you through choosing and setting them up, so the site is fully yours.

How long does it take?

3–5 working days from when we have your content: your logo, photos, text and contact details.

What if I want changes?

You get 2 rounds of revisions. You see the design before it goes live, so you can tell us what to adjust.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Once it's live, we show you how to change your text and photos, so you're not stuck waiting on us for small edits.
