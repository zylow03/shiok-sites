FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything people usually ask before getting started. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features you need. Tell us about your business and we'll send a clear quote before any work starts. Domain and hosting are not included in the build: you can buy and set them up yourself, or we can do it for an additional charge. A Shiok Care plan also includes them, along with renewals and edits.

Who will I be dealing with?

Our client manager. She'll chat with you on WhatsApp, collect everything we need for your website, and keep you updated until launch, while our designer builds your site.

How long does it take?

3–5 working days from when we have your content: your logo, photos, text and contact details.

What if I want changes?

You get 2 rounds of revisions. You see the design before it goes live, so you can tell us what to adjust.

How do I make changes after launch?

Just send us the change on WhatsApp. Edits are included in the Shiok Care plans, and one-off changes outside a plan are quoted upfront before we start.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The website is yours to keep. If we register a domain for you, it is registered in your name, even if you later stop using our care plan.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Not as part of the build. We hand over your finished website, and you can host it with any provider you like. If you'd like us to set up your domain and hosting, that's available for an additional charge, or included in a Shiok Care plan along with renewals.

Do I have to sign up for a care plan?

No. Care plans are optional and have no lock-in. If you'd rather manage things yourself, we'll hand over your website files.

Can you add bookings, ordering or enquiry forms?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons, enquiry forms and simple booking or ordering flows are all possible. Tell us what you need and we'll tell you honestly what fits.

Ready for a website you're proud of?

Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram and tell us what your business does.
